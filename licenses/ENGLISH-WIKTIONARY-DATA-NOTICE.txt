LingKuma modifications: select English entries with Mandarin/Chinese
translations, preserve exact-surface English pronunciation-only records,
normalize translations to Simplified Chinese with OpenCC t2s, filter malformed
target-language records, deduplicate values, retain bounded
lexical/POS/form/pronunciation data, and convert the result to the LingKuma
SQLite schema. The derived dictionary is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. No
English Wiktionary or Kaikki endorsement is implied.

Wikimedia audio files are not bundled in the dictionary. Pronunciation records
contain HTTPS Wikimedia Commons URLs and per-file Commons source-page URLs.
Each remote media file remains subject to the author and license shown on its
own Commons file page.
